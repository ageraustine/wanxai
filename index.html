Selected work

A few things we've built

Multi-agent reinforcement learning research and production agentic tooling — a look at how we approach hard problems.

Agentic Systems

AIDAW

An agentic, petabyte-scale media engine — a supervisor agent coordinates dedicated ffmpeg, search and data agents, each backed by its own LLM, to process media at scale from a single CLI.

Multi-Agent RL

MARL Football

Teams of agents learn coordinated play from scratch via self-play — passing, positioning and marking emerge without being hand-coded.

Multi-Agent RL

MARL Mahjong

Agents trained under imperfect information and a large combinatorial action space — a harder coordination and inference problem than most game environments.
